## Details

### UC1 · FOIA intake triage and record search
- **Owner:** FOIA Office
- **Baseline:** 2,100 requests/yr; median 74 days for complex track; backlog of 380
- **Human decision point:** FOIA officer makes every search, exemption and release decision
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for CUI
- **Notes:** Statutory deadlines and a backlog give a clear metric. Prototype in this repo.

### UC2 · Grant application completeness check
- **Owner:** Program offices
- **Baseline:** 6,000 applications/yr; 30% returned for missing items
- **Human decision point:** Program staff confirm every deficiency notice
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for INTERNAL
- **Notes:** High volume, checklist-shaped task, low harm if wrong because staff confirm.

### UC4 · Staff Q&A over grants policy and guidance
- **Owner:** Office of Policy
- **Baseline:** ~900 internal policy questions/month to the policy inbox
- **Human decision point:** Answers cite source guidance; staff remain responsible
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for PUBLIC
- **Notes:** Fastest to ship; public content only. Good first win while UC1 goes through review.

### UC3 · Grantee report summarization and anomaly flags
- **Owner:** Office of Grant Oversight
- **Baseline:** 1,600 quarterly reports; reviewed by sampling only
- **Human decision point:** Grant officers decide on follow-up
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for INTERNAL
- **Notes:** Report formats vary; needs a data-normalization step first.

### UC5 · Drafting replies to public inquiries
- **Owner:** Office of Public Affairs
- **Baseline:** 12,000 inquiries/yr; 9-day average response
- **Human decision point:** Staff edit and send every reply
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for INTERNAL
- **Notes:** Tone and accuracy review needed; reuse the UC4 knowledge base.

### UC6 · Legacy grants portal code modernization
- **Owner:** Office of the CIO
- **Baseline:** 180k lines of legacy code; modernization contract underway (FGA-010)
- **Human decision point:** Engineers review and merge every change
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for INTERNAL
- **Notes:** Coding agents (e.g. Codex) with the systems integrator; needs the SI's repos and CI.

### UC8 · Meeting summaries for review panels
- **Owner:** Program offices
- **Baseline:** ~60 panels/yr
- **Human decision point:** Panel chair approves summaries
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for INTERNAL
- **Notes:** Easy, but low mission value; bundle with an enterprise rollout rather than build bespoke.

### UC7 · Improper-payment risk scoring
- **Owner:** Office of Inspector General
- **Baseline:** 1.8% estimated improper payment rate
- **Human decision point:** Investigators decide; model output could affect rights
- **Endpoint needed:** authorized for CUI
- **Notes:** High value but rights-impacting; needs a formal impact assessment and much more data work. Phase 3 at earliest.
