CUI//SP-CTI. Between May 12 and May 16, 2025, attackers sent phishing emails impersonating the grants portal to 340 grantee contacts. Two accounts were compromised and reset. Detection rules and network indicators are listed in Annex B. The incident was reported to CISA.
