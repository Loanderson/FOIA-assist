date: 2025-01-30
office: Water Infrastructure Program Office
type: guidance
marking: PUBLIC
status: final
---
FGA will award up to $60 million for drinking water and wastewater treatment upgrades in communities under 10,000 people. Applications are due April 30, 2025. Scoring criteria: public health need (40 points), project readiness (30), cost effectiveness (20), and cost-share (10).
