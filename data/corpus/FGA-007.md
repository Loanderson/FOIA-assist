---
id: FGA-007
title: Final policy: revised cost-share match for persistent-poverty counties
date: 2025-05-01
office: Office of Policy
type: guidance
marking: PUBLIC
status: final
---
Effective for fiscal year 2026 awards, applicants located in persistent-poverty counties may meet a reduced 10 percent cost-share match. All other applicants remain subject to the 25 percent match. In-kind contributions may count toward up to half of the match.
