id: FGA-002
title: Grant reporting requirements, fiscal year 2025
date: 2025-01-15
office: Office of Grant Oversight
type: guidance
marking: PUBLIC
status: final
---
All recipients of FGA grants must submit quarterly financial reports within 30 days of quarter end and an annual performance report within 90 days of the fiscal year end. Late reports may result in payment holds. This guidance applies to the Rural Broadband and Water Infrastructure programs.
