The Federal Grants Administration today announced 18 awards totaling $42 million under the 2025 Rural Broadband Grant Program. Awards will expand high-speed internet access to an estimated 31,000 rural households across nine states. Recipients include county governments, tribal entities and rural electric cooperatives. Media contact: press@fga.example.gov.
