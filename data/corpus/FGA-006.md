PREDECISIONAL AND DELIBERATIVE. This draft presents three options for revising the 25 percent cost-share match for rural applicants: (1) keep the current match, (2) reduce it to 10 percent for persistent-poverty counties, (3) allow in-kind contributions. Staff recommend option 2, pending legal review.
